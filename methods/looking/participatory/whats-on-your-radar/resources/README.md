# What's on Your Radar — Resources

## What's Here

| File | Contents | Best used in |
|---|---|---|
| `tips.md` | Practitioner wisdom, facilitation gotchas, things that trip people up | Coaching, Facilitating |
| `faq.md` | Questions that come up repeatedly | Coaching, Teaching |
| `ai-usage.md` | AI prompt for generating items, guidance for AI-assisted and simulated use | Executing |
| `recipes.md` | How this method connects to and sequences with other methods | Coaching |
| `references.md` | External reading and related frameworks | Teaching |
| `visuals/` | Radar template sketches and visual references | Teaching, Facilitating |
| `templates/` | Digital and printable radar templates | Facilitating, Executing |
| `examples/` | Real session examples and filled-in artifacts | Teaching, Coaching |

## Agent Mode Guidance

- **Coaching:** Start with `tips.md` and `recipes.md` — they help you diagnose what someone needs and how this method sequences with others.
- **Teaching:** Use `teaching.md` (parent folder) as your primary content. Supplement with `faq.md` and `visuals/`.
- **Facilitating:** Pull up `mechanics.md` as your primary reference. Keep `tips.md` accessible for in-session decisions.
- **Executing:** Use `ai-usage.md` for the generation prompt. Refer to `templates/` for artifacts.
- **Participating:** Keep `mechanics.md` visible so you understand where the group is in the process.
