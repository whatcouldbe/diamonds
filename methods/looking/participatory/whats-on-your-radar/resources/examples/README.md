## Accessible Demo Scenarios

When teaching or demonstrating What's on Your Radar, the directive needs to be universally relatable — something everyone in the room has a genuine opinion about regardless of their role or function. Internal process scenarios work well: they're experienced across the business and generate real responses across all four default categories (Vision, Impact, People, Challenges).

These scenarios share a common structure: a team has been asked to gather views, leadership wants to understand before acting, and participants contribute as themselves. This models the research posture the method is designed to support.

---

**Internal: Travel & Expenses**

Your team has been asked to gather views from colleagues across the business on their experience of the travel and expenses process. Before any changes are made, leadership wants to understand what's actually on people's minds — what's working, what's getting in the way, and what feels most important to fix. Participants can simply be themselves.

*Opportunity: Improve the travel and expenses experience for people across the business.*

*Provenance example: This opportunity comes from a recent employee survey — travel and expenses came up repeatedly as a pain point.*
