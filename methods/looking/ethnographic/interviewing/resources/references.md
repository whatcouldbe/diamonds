# Interviewing — References

Curated external resources for going deeper on research interviewing. Useful in Teaching mode for further learning and in Coaching mode when someone wants to read more before conducting interviews.

---

## Foundational Reading

**[Interviewing Users: How to Uncover Compelling Insights](https://rosenfeldmedia.com/books/interviewing-users/) — Steve Portigal (Rosenfeld Media)**
The definitive practitioner book on research interviewing. Covers the full arc from preparation through synthesis, with emphasis on the listening posture and the skills that separate a productive interview from a wasted conversation.

**[How Many Participants for a UX Interview?](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/interview-sample-size/) — Nielsen Norman Group**
Addresses the most common question practitioners ask. Covers saturation, diminishing returns, and the practical guidance that 5–7 interviews per round is typically sufficient. Essential reading for teams that want to over-recruit.

## Related Frameworks

**[Contextual Inquiry](https://www.luma-institute.com/contextual-inquiry/) — LUMA Institute**
LUMA's reference for contextual inquiry — the hybrid method that combines interviewing with observation in the interviewee's environment. Good comparator for when teams are deciding between a standard interview and a contextual approach.

**[Interviewing](https://www.luma-institute.com/interviewing/) — LUMA Institute**
LUMA's reference for the interviewing method. Useful comparator for practitioners who may have encountered the method through a LUMA training.
