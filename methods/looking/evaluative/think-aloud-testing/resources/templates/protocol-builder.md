# Think-Aloud Testing — Protocol Builder

Use this before your first session to write your own moderation script. Fill in the sentence starters below, then read it aloud once before you begin. It's a guide — not a word-for-word recitation. Adapt in the moment.

If you'd prefer AI to draft it for you, see the prompt in `../ai-usage.md`.

---

## Your Script

**Open**
Hello, my name is _______________________________________________

We are _______________________________________________

This is _______________________________________________

**Get consent to record**
Before we start, I'd like to record the screen and your voice. Is that okay?

**Set them at ease**
I want to remind you: we are NOT testing you. There are no wrong answers. And I didn't make this, so you genuinely can't offend me.

**Explain think-aloud**
The one thing I'll ask is that you put your brain on speakerphone — narrate everything as you go. What you're looking at, what you expect, what's confusing. We call it a think-aloud test.

**Give the task(s)**
What I would like you to do is _______________________________________________

_(Give one task at a time. Add additional tasks below as needed.)_

**Check in**
Any questions before we get started?

**Close**
Thank you _______________________________________________

## Tips

- Introduce yourself and the purpose clearly — participants are more honest when they know who you are and why you're here
- Remind each participant: "We are NOT testing you." Say it explicitly; don't assume they'll infer it
- Make the think-aloud instruction land — say it clearly, and consider miming it (hand on top of the head, thoughts streaming out)
- Give one task at a time; don't list them all upfront
- Once the task starts: keep quiet, listen carefully, and take notes. The hardest discipline in the method is not jumping in to explain
- Thank each participant genuinely — their narration is a gift

## Before Your First Session

- Read your script aloud at least once
- Do a dry run with a colleague to catch technical issues (recording not capturing, prototype breaking, link not working)
- The script is a guide. Adapt as needed in the moment — what matters is the moves, not the words
