# Critique — References

Curated external resources for going deeper on Critique. Useful in Teaching mode for pointing people to further learning, and in Coaching mode when someone wants to read more before running the method.

---

## Foundational Reading

**[Design Critiques: Encourage a Positive Culture to Improve Products](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/design-critiques/) — Nielsen Norman Group**
The most widely cited practical guide to running design critiques. Covers the structure, roles, and culture conditions that make critique productive. Required reading before facilitating your first formal crit.

**[What are Design Critiques?](https://www.interaction-design.org/literature/topics/design-critiques) — Interaction Design Foundation**
Annotated overview of design critique with bibliography. Good entry point for someone new to the method who wants both practical guidance and links to deeper academic work.

**[How to Run a UX Design Critique](https://jakobnielsenphd.substack.com/p/design-crit) — Jakob Nielsen**
Practitioner-focused guide from a foundational UX voice. Covers when to critique, who to invite, and how to structure the conversation.

---

## Video

**[UX Design Critiques: 3 Tips for Effective Feedback](https://www.nngroup.com/videos/tips-for-effective-feedback/) — Nielsen Norman Group**
Short video covering the three moves that make feedback in a critique session more useful: valuing your perspective, structuring feedback, and balancing positive and critical input.

## Advanced & Academic

**[A Systematic Literature Review of the Design Critique Method](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0950584922001902) — ScienceDirect (2022)**
Academic review of how design critique is used in HCI and UX research. Proposes a generic process for conducting critique studies. Useful for practitioners who want research grounding for the method's validity.

---

**[Critique](https://www.luma-institute.com/critique/) — LUMA Institute**
LUMA's reference for the Critique method. Their framing emphasizes that effective feedback must be "audible, credible, and actionable" — a useful touchstone for facilitators. LUMA is one of the most widely-used HCD method libraries; their version of Critique shares the same structure (warm/positive → cool/critical → suggestions) and is a good comparator to the WCB protocol.

---

## Related Methods & Frameworks

**[I Like, I Wish — Build Your Creative Confidence](https://www.ideo.com/journal/build-your-creative-confidence-i-like-i-wish) — IDEO**
IDEO's simpler, two-part feedback protocol — "I like..." and "I wish..." — that shares the same spirit as Critique but is faster and more informal. Good for quick feedback loops and teams new to structured feedback. Critique is more rigorous; I Like / I Wish is a lighter entry point.

**[4 Tricks for Getting Better Feedback](https://www.ideo.com/journal/4-tricks-for-getting-better-feedback) — IDEO**
Practical IDEO guidance on how to ask for and receive feedback in a way that produces useful input. Addresses the presenter's mindset — the same challenge the Time/Love Continuum speaks to.

**[Mastering Design Critiques](https://uxdesign.cc/design-critique-design-better-products-through-team-collaboration-4c4f26a7be5f) — UX Collective**
Practitioner guide with checklists and frameworks for running critiques that actually improve work. Good supplement to the protocol for teams building critique into their regular practice.

---

## Running Critique in Practice

**[How to Run a Design Critique That Actually Improves Work](https://www.thecrit.co/resources/how-to-run-design-critique) — The Crit**
Step-by-step practical guide with facilitation tips. Good reference for a first-time facilitator preparing to run a session.

**[Tips for a Productive Design Critique](https://www.toptal.com/designers/product-design/productive-design-critique-guide) — Toptal**
Covers the common failure modes — defensiveness, vague feedback, imbalanced critique — and how to prevent them. Useful pre-reading for teams that have tried critique before and found it unproductive.
