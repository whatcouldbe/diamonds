# Hypothesis Statements — References

This section is **grounded** — researched and cited. It is the firmest footing the reconstruction has.

## Lineage

Hypothesis Statements as supplied is a **Lean UX hypothesis statement** at its core, packaged as a fill-in template, with the measurable-threshold discipline of a **Strategyzer Test Card**, operating inside the **Lean Startup** build-measure-learn loop.

- **Lean UX (Jeff Gothelf & Josh Seiden).** The canonical format: *"We believe [doing/building X] for [these people] will achieve [outcome]. We will know this is true when we see [measurable signal]."* The supplied template's first two clauses map onto this directly.
- **Strategyzer Test Card (Alexander Osterwalder / Strategyzer).** Structure: *"We believe that [assumption]. To verify, we will [test]. And measure [metric]. We are right if [threshold]."* Source of the measurable-disproof discipline. The supplied template's "We'll know this is true when [quantitative impact]" is the Test Card's "we are right if [threshold]." → https://www.strategyzer.com (Test Card resource)
- **Lean Startup (Eric Ries).** The assumption → experiment → validated-learning loop these statements feed.

## The variant feature

The supplied template adds a clause not present in canonical Lean UX: **"We believe this will ultimately help improve [high-level goal]."** This ladders every testable bet up to a strategic end. It is the template's distinguishing contribution and the reason the method maps cleanly onto "begin and end with impact."

## Unconfirmed

- The specific **Mural** template packaging (layout, "See an example" affordance) appears to be a tool-library presentation of the above lineage. The exact origin of *this* template was not confirmed in research and is not asserted as canon.
