# Hypothesis Statements — Recipes

Per the Pathway B build rule, the rigor goes here: a method's sequencing drives when the agent surfaces it, regardless of which folder it sits in. This is the most load-bearing resource for this method.

## What it is (so the agent knows when to reach for it)

Hypothesis Statements is the **converter between making and validating**. Reach for it whenever a team has concepts and is about to spend effort building or testing them without having stated what would prove the bet wrong. The trigger is not a phase — it is the move from conviction to claim.

## What precedes it

- **Concept / ideation work** (Round Robin, Creative Matrix, concept posters) — produces the bets. Hypothesis Statements sharpens them into falsifiable form.
- **Problem framing** (Abstraction Laddering, How Might We) — when the bet is about a reframed problem, the hypothesis encodes the reframing as a testable claim.

## What follows it

- **Experiment / Test Card design** — each prioritised hypothesis's quantitative-impact clause becomes the experiment's success threshold. This is the primary downstream link.
- **Prototype testing / Think-Aloud Testing** — when the cheapest test of the hypothesis is a prototype in front of the target persona.
- **Interviewing** — when the disproof signal is qualitative before it is quantitative.

## The chain

```
Concepts → Hypothesis Statements → Experiment / Test Card → run → validated learning → revise or kill the bet
```

## Coaching note

If a team wants to skip from concept straight to building, this is the method to surface — not as a deliverable, but as the question: *what would tell us we're wrong, and have we written it down?* That question is the method's whole job.
