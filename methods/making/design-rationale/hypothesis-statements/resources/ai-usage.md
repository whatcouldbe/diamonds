# Hypothesis Statements — AI Usage

`[Reconstructed — inferred from the method's structure and lineage, not from practice.]`

## Where AI helps

- **Pressure-testing falsifiability.** Paste draft statements; ask the agent to flag any whose quantitative-impact clause could not come back negative. This is the highest-value AI use — it attacks the method's main failure mode.
- **Persona specificity check.** Ask whether each target persona is specific enough to recruit and interview. Vague personas get surfaced fast.
- **Ladder check.** Ask whether each high-level goal actually follows from its outcome or is a leap.

## Where AI must not substitute

- AI can draft plausible hypotheses, but a hypothesis the team did not commit to is not a bet — it is text. The conviction has to be the team's, or there is nothing real to validate. Use AI to *sharpen* the team's bets, not to *generate* bets the team then rationalises.

## Transparency

When the agent runs this method, it states that the method itself is reconstructed and unverified. Do not present AI-pressure-tested hypotheses as validated — they are still assumptions; the method only made them *testable*, not *tested*.
