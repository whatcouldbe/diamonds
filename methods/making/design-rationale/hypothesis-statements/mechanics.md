# Hypothesis Statements — Mechanics

> **Reconstructed and provisional.** The frame (Inputs/Outputs/Stuff) is grounded in the supplied template. The **Process** is inferred from lineage and general structured-template practice — it has not been taught or run. Say this before facilitating. See `../../../README.md` → Pathway B.

## Purpose

Turn the team's concepts into falsifiable, prioritised bets — each with a measurable disproof condition — before investing in building or testing them.

## Inputs

- Existing concepts or intended features (post-ideation)
- A named directive / concept space
- The statement frame, one row per hypothesis

## Outputs

- 3–5 prioritised, falsifiable hypothesis statements
- A success threshold per hypothesis (the quantitative-impact clause)
- A dated, attributed board ready to feed experiments

## Process `[reconstructed]`

1. **Frame** — name the directive the bets sit in.
2. **Generate** — individuals draft statements solo (avoid anchoring).
3. **Complete** — fill all five variables; an incomplete row is an opinion, not a hypothesis.
4. **Pressure-test** — falsifiable metric? findable persona? does it ladder?
5. **Converge** — prioritise by most load-bearing, least proven.
6. **Hand off** — each survivor → an experiment; its metric is the success threshold.

## Stuff

- The frame (board with parallel color-coded rows)
- Dated header, team attribution
- A timer `[reconstructed — no taught timing; ~60–90 min is an untested default]`

## In-the-moment

- Recurring question: *"What number would prove us wrong?"*
- Kill circular statements (feature = outcome).
- Don't let the high-level goal clause get rushed.
