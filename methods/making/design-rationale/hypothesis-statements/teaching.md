# Hypothesis Statements — Teaching

> **Reconstructed and provisional.** A real teaching arc carries a practitioner's story, lived edge cases, and a conviction close that cannot be reconstructed. This is scaffolding built from lineage and the worked example. When teaching, say plainly that the method is reconstructed and unverified. See `../../../README.md` → Pathway B.

## Hook

Two concepts on a whiteboard look identical: one the team is sure about, one they've actually tested. Confidence and validation are indistinguishable until you write the bet as a sentence that could be proven wrong. This method is that sentence.

## Key insight

A hypothesis is not a prediction you defend — it's a claim you've built a way to lose. The measurable-impact clause isn't decoration; it's the disproof condition. A statement that can't come back disappointing isn't a hypothesis, it's an opinion in a nicer font.

Walk the five variables using the FreshMeals example (`resources/examples/freshmeals.md`) — show the blank frame, then the filled rows. The contrast is the lesson. Draw out: every metric has a number and a direction; every persona is recruitable; the high-level goal answers "so what."

## Why it belongs in Diamonds

This is the central principle made into an artifact: everything is an assumption until tested. The method's whole job is to stop a team moving from conviction to investment without writing down what would change their mind.
