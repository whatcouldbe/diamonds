# Value Proposition Poster — References & Lineage

The Value Proposition Poster is a practitioner-evolved format with three traceable ancestors. Name them honestly when teaching — the method is a synthesis, not an invention from nothing.

## Concept Poster (Luma Institute)

The direct ancestor. In the Luma System of design methods, the **Concept Poster** is a presentation format for illustrating the main points of a new idea on a single sheet. It sits in the "Concept Ideation" / making space. There are many ways to lay out a concept poster, with more or less detail; Luma offers a standard articulation of the key elements.

The Value Proposition Poster is a more opinionated, value-led descendant — it fixes the structure around a value proposition statement, a customer storyboard, and an explicit assumptions zone.

*Source: Luma Institute, Innovating for People / the LUMA System of Innovation methods.*

## Value Proposition Canvas (Strategyzer)

The **Value Proposition Canvas**, created by Alexander Osterwalder and colleagues at Strategyzer (companion to the Business Model Canvas), is the rigorous tool for articulating how a product creates value — mapping customer jobs, pains, and gains against pain relievers and gain creators.

It is rigorous but many people find it hard to interpret on sight. The Value Proposition Poster's single-sentence statement is designed to do a more *legible* job of presenting a value proposition to a non-specialist, busy audience — while borrowing the canvas's underlying logic of jobs, pains, and gains.

*Source: Osterwalder, Pigneur, Bernarda, Smith — "Value Proposition Design" (Strategyzer / Wiley).*

## Field evolution (What Could Be)

The Value Proposition Poster as taught here is the result of What Could Be applying and adapting the Concept Poster with large enterprises. The recurring need was a format that conveyed a proposition faster and more memorably to busy decision-makers — the executives who choose what to back. The poster has been taught many times and is embedded in some organisations' own internal ways of working.

Key adaptations WCB introduced over the Concept Poster:
- A fixed **value proposition statement** structure (the *Our [X] helps [who] who want to [job] by [pain] and [gain]* mad-lib)
- A six-question **customer storyboard** as the "how it works" zone
- An explicit **desirability / feasibility / viability** assumptions zone that admits what isn't known yet
- A built-in handoff to **Critique** and to choosing the first element to **prototype**
