# Statement Starters — Recipes

How Statement Starters connects to other methods. Use this when sequencing a programme, planning a workshop arc, or helping a team understand where this method sits in the process.

---

## What Feeds Statement Starters

**Affinity Clustering → Statement Starters**
The most natural sequence. Affinity Clustering takes raw research data and organises it into themes and insights. Those insights are the ideal input for a How Might We statement — each insight points to a human need, a tension, or an unmet expectation that can be reframed as a design challenge. The transition is often immediate: if an insight already begins with a verb ("People struggle to..."), the HMW reframe follows directly.

Statement Starters can feel so obvious after Affinity Clustering that teams skip the step — they go straight from insight to ideation. Don't. The act of consciously reframing the insight as a HMW question is what shifts the team from diagnosis mode into generative mode, and the quality of the question shapes the quality of what follows.

**Abstraction Laddering → Statement Starters**
When a team has used Abstraction Laddering to find the right level of challenge, the chosen framing translates directly into a HMW statement. The ladder has already done the work of finding the right altitude; Statement Starters adds the verb, the context, and the people — making it actionable as an ideation prompt.

**Interviewing → Statement Starters**
Field research produces observations about human behaviour, needs, and frustrations. These observations can be translated directly into HMW statements, particularly when a team is moving quickly from research to ideation without a full synthesis phase. Less rich than the Affinity Clustering route, but viable for faster-moving work.

## What Follows Statement Starters

**Statement Starters → Round Robin**
The HMW statement is the prompt that Round Robin responds to. Each participant generates ideas in response to the same question. A sharp, provocative HMW statement produces a wider, more interesting range of responses. Run Statement Starters immediately before Round Robin when the team is ready to ideate — the transition should be seamless.

**Statement Starters → Creative Matrix**
The HMW statement provides the challenge that the Creative Matrix is built to explore. The horizontal axis of the matrix names different lenses or constraints; every cell in the matrix is an attempt to answer the same HMW question through a different lens. A well-formed statement is a prerequisite for a Creative Matrix that generates genuinely different directions rather than variations on one obvious answer.

---

## Common Sequences

| Sequence | When to use |
|---|---|
| Affinity Clustering → Statement Starters → Round Robin | Standard synthesis-to-ideation arc: insights → design challenge → ideas |
| Affinity Clustering → Statement Starters → Creative Matrix | When you want structured ideation across multiple lenses rather than open generation |
| Abstraction Laddering → Statement Starters → Round Robin | When scope alignment is needed before ideation begins |
| Interviewing → Statement Starters → Round Robin | Fast track: field research directly into ideation, skipping full synthesis |
