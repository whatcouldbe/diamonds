# Abstraction Laddering — Recipes

How this method fits into larger sequences. Abstraction laddering is almost always an opening move — it sets direction before other methods execute on it.

---

## Recipe 1 — Project Kickoff Alignment

**Challenge:** A team has been handed a directive and needs to align on scope and direction before work begins.

**Sequence:**
1. **Abstraction Laddering** — Surface the WHYs and HOWs. Choose the right framing level.
2. **How Might We** — Convert the chosen framing into design challenges.
3. **Creative Matrix** — Use the HMW questions and the HOW clusters as axes for ideation.

**Why this works:** The ladder establishes the goals and the design space. HMW translates that into actionable challenges. Creative Matrix generates solutions grounded in both.

---

## Recipe 2 — Research Framing

**Challenge:** A team has completed discovery research and needs to frame their findings into a clear opportunity before moving into definition.

**Sequence:**
1. **Affinity Clustering** — Synthesize research findings into themes.
2. **Abstraction Laddering** — Take the most important theme and ladder it to find the right framing.
3. **How Might We** — Convert the chosen framing into design challenges to drive the next phase.

**Why this works:** Affinity clustering finds the patterns in the data; abstraction laddering frames the most important pattern at the right altitude; HMW turns it into a design challenge.

---

## Recipe 3 — Solo Brief Preparation

**Challenge:** You're preparing a brief, proposal, or project plan and want to make sure it's framed at the right level before sharing it.

**Sequence:**
1. **Abstraction Laddering** (solo) — Generate WHYs and HOWs individually, or use the AI prompt. Choose the level.
2. **How Might We** — Convert the chosen framing into one or two clear design challenges.
3. Share the brief with the chosen framing and HMW questions as the anchoring statement.

**Why this works:** The solo version of the method sharpens your own thinking before you ask others to engage with it. Coming to a stakeholder conversation with a clear framing — and the alternatives you considered — builds credibility.

---

## Natural Pairings

| Method | Relationship |
|---|---|
| **How Might We** | Almost always follows abstraction laddering — converts the chosen framing into a design challenge |
| **Affinity Clustering** | Often precedes abstraction laddering when the input is research findings rather than a given directive |
| **Creative Matrix** | Follows when the team is ready to ideate — uses the chosen framing and HOW clusters as starting points |
| **Importance/Difficulty Matrix** | Can be used to evaluate the HOWs — which approaches are most worth pursuing given effort and impact |
| **Interviewing** | Can precede abstraction laddering (to gather input) or follow it (to validate the chosen framing with real people) |
