# Abstraction Laddering — Templates

Ready-to-use templates for running the method in digital and physical environments.

---

## What Belongs Here

- **Miro template** — the primary digital facilitation tool. Pre-built with three frames: (1) WHY generation workspace (yellow stickies, "In order to..." starter, space by participant), (2) HOW generation workspace (purple stickies, "Enabled by..." starter, space by participant), (3) shared ladder diagram with directive in center, arrows showing WHY/HOW directions, clustering space above and below
- **Printable template** — a print-ready version of the ladder for in-person sessions
- **FigJam or Mural version** — if created

---

## Miro Template Structure

The Miro board is organized into three frames that map to the flow of the session:

**Frame 1 — WHY Generation**
Title: *"Frame the challenge more broadly by asking, 'Why? Why else?'"*
Each participant has a labeled workspace with yellow sticky notes pre-loaded with "In order to..." starter text. Individual generation happens here before moving to the shared ladder.

**Frame 2 — HOW Generation**
Title: *"Frame the challenge more narrowly by asking, 'How? How else?'"*
Each participant has a labeled workspace with purple/blue sticky notes pre-loaded with "Enabled by..." starter text. Individual generation happens here before moving to the shared ladder.

**Frame 3 — Shared Ladder**
Title: *"Make sure you're focused on the right problem — using Abstraction Laddering"*
Center: A prominent yellow sticky labeled "State your directive. 'We need to...'"
Above: Arrows pointing up labeled "Why?" and "Why else?" with space for WHY stickies at two levels
Below: Arrows pointing down labeled "How?" and "How else?" with space for HOW stickies at two levels
Instructions (top right): Have one person describe, then place, an item. Invite others to place similar items in proximity. Repeat until all items are included. Discuss and rearrange as groupings emerge. Label the clusters that take shape.
