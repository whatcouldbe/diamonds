## Notes for Teaching Mode

The most useful visual for teaching this method is the sketch Justin drew in the video — a simple ladder with clouds at the top (goals/purpose) and grass at the bottom (tactics), with the directive sitting in the middle. This image communicates the core idea in seconds: you're climbing up toward purpose and descending toward action, and the question is where you should be standing.

When teaching in person, drawing this live — as Justin does in the video — is more effective than showing a polished template. The act of drawing grounds the abstraction (pun intended) and gives learners something to recreate for themselves.
