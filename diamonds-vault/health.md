## Q1 — What goals do we need to achieve, and how will we know we've achieved them?

**Status: Partially validated**

**What's confirmed:**
- The problem is real and client-validated: people cannot apply HCD independently after training; they need a practitioner present. This is a known gap in existing client relationships.
- A recent unsolicited client brief — "How might we best utilize AI to optimize the day-to-day work of the design experience team" — confirms the problem is live and being felt now.
- The goal is directionally clear: scale HCD practice without scaling the people. Make decades of design expertise available at the moment of need, self-service.
- This ambition predates Diamonds — it was a funded project goal at What Could Be before the tools existed to make it possible.

**What's still open:**
- No specific success metric defined. "People applying HCD independently without needing us" is directionally right but not yet measurable.
- No threshold or signal established for what "working at scale" looks like.

**Seven selection criteria:**
1. Relatable — recognizable problem space for likely adopters
2. Full process — spans multiple phases, shows Diamonds navigating real decisions
3. Completable — reaches a meaningful endpoint
4. Artifacts worth showing — vault, log, health file tell the story
5. Real stakes — genuine work, not synthetic
6. Energizing — both Justin and David are genuinely excited
7. Transferable context — someone outside What Could Be can follow and recognize their situation
