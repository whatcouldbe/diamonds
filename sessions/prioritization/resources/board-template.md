## Pre-Session Setup

Fill these in before participants arrive:
- The driving question (PURPOSE frame)
- The date (PURPOSE frame)
- The source of the items being prioritized (CONFIRM ITEMS frame)
- One item per sticky note — 8–10 items maximum. If you have more than 12, run a Bullseye Diagramming activity first to narrow the set.

---

## Progressive Reveal Sequence

The working frame uses a layered reveal — the difficulty axis and quadrant labels are hidden until the right moment. This is the most critical mechanic to understand before facilitating.

**Why it matters:** If participants see the quadrants before they've ranked both dimensions, they start arguing for quadrant placement instead of having honest importance and difficulty conversations. The reveal is what keeps the discussion clean.

**How it works in the template:** The working area has four images stacked on top of each other. Delete the top image at the end of each stage to reveal the next one.

| Stage | What's visible | When to advance |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Importance axis only | After all items are ranked by importance and spread evenly left to right |
| 2 | Both axes, difficulty criteria shown | After all items are ranked by difficulty and spread evenly top to bottom |
| 3 | Completed matrix, no quadrant labels | Pause here — let participants observe the pattern before naming the quadrants |
| 4 | Completed matrix with quadrant labels | End state — discuss the pattern and draft the road map |

## Adapting for Your Organization

If your organization has a branded template in any of these tools, use that instead — the layout and facilitation flow are the same. Only the visual styling needs to change.

For groups larger than 8, split into two parallel groups each with their own copy of the working frame. Reconvene to compare and synthesize.
